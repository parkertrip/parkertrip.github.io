Recommended distance: 3 meters (10 feet)

This is the standard distance for most online eye tests and works well on all screen sizes.

To take the eye test:

  • Sit 3 meters (10 feet) from your screen.
  • Cover one eye at a time and read the letters on the chart below.
  • Note the smallest line you can read clearly.
Consulting a doctor is recommended for professional medical advice.
