The System

Vocal Command

"Hey Yappey, open YouTube." Seamless cloud-bridged voice control for your home device. The onboard mic captures your request with precision.

Cloud Bridge

Link to your devices from miles away. The WiFi cloud integration keeps your logic connected 24/7, bridging your phone to your remote setup.

Display Logic

Integrated AR feedback projected directly through the optical prism shows you exactly what's happening on your device in real-time.

Hardware Core

The Pen Case Enclosure

Subtle, minimalist 3D-printed enclosure. Holds the processing core, battery, and optical hardware in a sleek profile.

ESP32 Neural Node

Powered by an ESP32 micro-controller flashed via Arduino IDE. Optimized for low-latency Bluetooth pairing to your phone and WiFi cloud handshakes.

T-Glass Optics

Utilizes advanced T-Glass hardware. The optical prism clips seamlessly onto any standard pair of glasses, delivering crisp AR visuals.

Technical Specifications

Engineered for lightweight performance and all-day connectivity.

Core Processor

ESP32 Micro-controller for robust Bluetooth/WiFi bridging.

Optical Display

LilyGO T-Glass Prism display for clear, non-obtrusive augmented reality.

Power Source

200mAh Lithium Polymer (LiPo) battery with integrated USB-C charging.

Audio Interface

Built-in microphone tuned for "Hey Yappie" wake-word recognition.
